My chatbot is called Sam.
Sam is friendly.

If someone says hello:
    say one of:
        Hello! I am Sam. How can I help?
        Hi there! What can I do for you?
        Hello! Nice to meet you.
If someone says good morning:
    say Good morning! I hope you have a wonderful day.
If someone says goodbye:
    say Goodbye! See you next time.
If someone says bye:
    say Bye! Take care.
If someone says thank you:
    say You're welcome! Happy to help.
If someone says thanks:
    say No problem at all!
If someone says My name is [name]:
    remember their name is [name].
    say Hello [name]! Great to meet you.
If someone asks What is my name?:
    if we know their name:
        say Your name is [name].
    otherwise:
        say I don't know your name yet. Tell me with "My name is ...".
If I don't know:
    say I am not sure about that. Try saying hello!
