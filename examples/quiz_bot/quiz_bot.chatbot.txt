My chatbot is called Quiz Bot.
Quiz Bot is cheerful.

If someone says hello:
    say Hello! Welcome to the quiz. Type "start quiz" to begin!
If someone says start quiz:
    say Great! Here is your first question.
    say Question 1: What is 5 + 5?
    say Type your answer!
If someone says 10:
    say Correct! Well done!
    say Question 2: What is the capital of France?
If someone says Paris:
    say Correct! Paris is the capital of France.
    say Question 3: What do you call a baby dog?
If someone says puppy:
    say Correct! A baby dog is called a puppy.
    say Well done! You finished the quiz.
If someone says I don't know:
    say That's okay! Have another try.
If I don't know:
    say I am not sure about that answer. Try again!
